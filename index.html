Podcast Player

Podcast Player is a personal, accessible podcast player for Windows, built for one blind listener who uses it with the NVDA screen reader. It runs on his own computer and is not offered to anyone else.

It keeps his listening in step with Pocket Casts. For podcast episodes that come from YouTube videos, it plays them through YouTube's own embedded player. When he presses a key, it can also like or dislike the video he is listening to, using the YouTube Data API on his own YouTube account.
